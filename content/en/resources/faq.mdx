# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is X-Agent?
X-Agent is a zero-code launchpad that lets anyone build, deploy, and monetize custom AI Agents using pure natural language, rooted in Web3 social graphs.

## Do I need to know how to code?
No. You describe what you want in plain language and X-Agent generates a working Agent for you — no programming or server setup required.

## Where can I deploy my Agent?
To Telegram, WhatsApp, or a standard web interface, with a single click.

## What is $XAGT used for?
$XAGT is the ecosystem's gas and commission settlement layer — it powers computation, staking, and settles commercial transactions brokered by Agents. See [Tokenomics](/en/litepaper).

## How can I earn?
Through three-dimensional Business Mining — running Agents (Execution), sharing and converting new users (Distribution), and building premium Agents that generate revenue (Value).

Token generation and listing details will be announced. Watch the [Changelog](/en/changelog) for updates.
