## Name and tagline

Always write the name as **X-Agent**, including the hyphen. Do not use “XAgent” or “Xagent.”

> Speak to Build. Share to Connect.

## Usage

- Use the supplied artwork as-is. Do not redraw, distort, recolor, crop, or add effects to the logo.
- Choose the variant that maintains clear contrast with its background.
- For additional formats or partnership materials, use [Contact & Support](/en/resources/contact).
