# How It Works

X-Agent turns a plain-language request into a deployed, shareable AI Agent in two moves — no code, no servers.

## 1. Speak to Build

Describe what you want in natural language. X-Agent's engine parses your intent, aggregates the right models, and configures a custom workflow — instantly generating a specialized, function-rich Agent. No programming required.

## 2. Share to Connect

Deploy your Agent to Telegram, WhatsApp, or the web with one click. Powered by Web3 social protocols, your Agent recognizes your real-world network and can automatically connect with other users' Agents that share the same demand — turning isolated tools into a living social and commercial graph.

## The flow

**Describe → Generate → Deploy → Share → Connect &amp; Earn**

Every Agent you build can be distributed across the network, and both building and sharing are rewarded through $XAGT [Business Mining](/en/litepaper).
